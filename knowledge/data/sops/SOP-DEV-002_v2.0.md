# SOP-DEV-002 Deviation Classification and Risk Assessment (v2.0)

## 1 Purpose

This procedure sets the criteria for classifying deviations as minor, major or critical.

## 2 Scope

All deviations raised under SOP-DEV-001.

## 3 Classification criteria

Critical: confirmed or probable impact on patient safety, or failure of a critical quality attribute that reached or could reach distribution. Major: potential impact on product quality or a repeat of a minor event. Minor: no impact on product quality and no regulatory implication.

## 4 Risk assessment

QA scores severity, likelihood of occurrence and likelihood of detection from 1 to 3 and multiplies them. A score of 18 or more is critical, 8 to 17 major, below 8 minor, unless a criterion in section 3 applies directly.

## 5 Recurrence

QA checks the deviation history for the same event type, equipment or product in the last 24 months. A recurrence of a minor event is classified at least major.

## 6 Reclassification

Reclassification needs a documented reason and QA approval and is recorded in the deviation record.
