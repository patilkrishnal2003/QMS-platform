# SOP-CHG-001 Change Control (v2.0)

## 1 Purpose

This procedure controls changes to equipment, processes, specifications, systems and documents that could affect product quality.

## 2 Scope

Permanent and temporary planned changes at all sites.

## 3 Change request

The requester describes the change, the reason and the affected products, equipment and documents.

## 4 Assessment and approval

QA and affected functions assess impact under SOP-CHG-002. The quality unit approves the change before implementation. Emergency changes need retrospective approval within five working days.

## 5 Implementation

The change is implemented only after approval. Training, document updates and any required validation or calibration updates are completed first.

## 6 Closure

QA closes the change when all actions are complete and verification shows the change had the intended effect.

## 7 References

21 CFR 211.22 Responsibilities of quality control unit. 21 CFR 211.68 Automatic, mechanical, and electronic equipment.
