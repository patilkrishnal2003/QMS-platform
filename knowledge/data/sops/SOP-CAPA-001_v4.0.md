# SOP-CAPA-001 Corrective and Preventive Action (v4.0)

## 1 Purpose

This procedure defines how corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) are raised, approved, implemented and closed.

## 2 Scope

CAPAs arising from deviations, audits, complaints, trend reviews and management review.

## 3 Responsibilities

The CAPA owner implements the plan. QA approves the plan and the closure. The quality unit has final authority over CAPA closure.

## 4 CAPA initiation

A CAPA is raised when a root cause needs a systemic fix. A correction alone, such as re-cleaning or rework, does not need a CAPA but the decision is recorded in the deviation.

## 5 Action planning

Each action has an owner, a due date and a defined deliverable. Actions address the documented root cause and, where relevant, other products or equipment with the same weakness.

## 6 Implementation and closure

Actions are closed with objective evidence. The CAPA remains open until the effectiveness check of SOP-CAPA-002 is complete.
