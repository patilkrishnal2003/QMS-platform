# SOP-CHG-002 Change Impact and Risk Assessment (v1.0)

## 1 Purpose

This procedure defines how the impact and risk of a proposed change are assessed.

## 2 Scope

All change requests raised under SOP-CHG-001.

## 3 Impact checklist

The assessor checks effect on validated state, calibration and maintenance intervals, specifications, batch records, training, suppliers and regulatory commitments.

## 4 Risk rating

Each impact is rated low, medium or high. A high rating needs a mitigation plan approved by QA before implementation.

## 5 Interval and parameter changes

Changes to calibration, maintenance or alarm limits are changes and require assessment even when made to reduce workload.
