# SOP-DEV-001 Deviation Management (v3.0)

## 1 Purpose

This procedure defines how unplanned departures from approved procedures, specifications or parameters (deviations) are reported, contained, classified, investigated and closed at all Examplon sites.

## 2 Scope

Applies to manufacturing, packaging, laboratory, warehouse and utilities activities that can affect product quality. Planned temporary changes are handled under SOP-CHG-001, not as deviations.

## 3 Definitions

Deviation: any unplanned event that departs from an approved instruction or specification. Containment: immediate action to stop the event spreading to other product or equipment. Product impact: the assessed effect of the event on identity, strength, quality or purity.

## 4 Responsibilities

The originator reports the event. The area supervisor confirms containment. Quality Assurance (QA) classifies the deviation, approves the investigation and decides disposition of affected lots.

## 5 Procedure

The sub-sections below are followed in order.

## 5.1 Detection and initial report

The originator records the event in the deviation record within 24 hours of detection, including event type, site, product, lot, equipment, event date, detection date and a factual description.

## 5.2 Immediate action and containment

The supervisor records the immediate action taken, for example quarantining affected lots, stopping the equipment or segregating material, before the end of the shift.

## 5.3 Classification

QA classifies the deviation as minor, major or critical within two working days using SOP-DEV-002. Classification may be revised if the investigation shows a different risk.

## 5.4 Investigation

The investigation follows SOP-INV-001. Major and critical deviations require a cross-functional team and a documented root cause.

## 5.5 Product and batch impact assessment

QA documents whether other lots, products or equipment could be affected and decides the disposition of every affected lot (release, rework or reject).

## 5.6 Closure

A deviation is closed only when the root cause is documented, the impact assessment is approved, any CAPA is raised under SOP-CAPA-001 and QA has signed the closure.

## 6 Timelines

Investigation target: critical 15 working days, major 30 working days, minor 45 working days from classification. Extensions need documented QA approval.

## 7 Records

The deviation record, attachments and approvals are retained under the records retention schedule and must be retrievable for batch record review and inspections.
