# SOP-CAPA-002 CAPA Effectiveness Verification (v1.0)

## 1 Purpose

This procedure defines how the effectiveness of a completed CAPA is verified.

## 2 Scope

All CAPAs closed under SOP-CAPA-001.

## 3 Effectiveness criteria

Criteria are written when the CAPA plan is approved and must be measurable, for example no recurrence of the same event type on the same equipment within six months.

## 4 Verification

QA reviews deviation and trend data after the defined period. The result is recorded as effective, effective with follow-up, or not effective.

## 5 Not effective

A CAPA found not effective is reopened or a new CAPA is raised with a revised root cause assessment.
